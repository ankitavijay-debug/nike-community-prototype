My Nike Run Club runs are not syncing to Apple Health. Anyone know why?

Common reasons Nike Run Club runs do not appear in Apple Health:

1. Permissions. Workouts access may be off. Enable it under Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Nike Run Club.
2. App version or sign-in. Update the app, then sign out and back in.
3. Sync delay. Data can take a few minutes to appear.

These fixes work for many people, but results vary. For reports from real runners, see the community.

Runs not syncing to Apple Health

Updated by Nike Support · Reviewed
  1. On your iPhone, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Nike Run Club and turn on Workouts.
  2. Update the app from the App Store.
  3. Sign out of the app, then sign back in.
  4. Wait a few minutes, then open Apple Health. Your run should appear.
